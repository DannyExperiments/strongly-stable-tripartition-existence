# Q621 literature and source check

**27 November 2026. High confidence in the exact target. Moderate confidence on priority after a bounded search.** The result classifies existence of a strongly stable three-level source ranking under polynomial-time many-one reductions. The checked sources supply the maxcon semantics and earlier special cases. No exact prior classification was found.

## Source and scope

- Liberatore, *Belief Integration and Source Reliability Assessment*, JAIR 63 (2018), 87–143, explicitly asks the existence complexity in §8.6, journal p. 137 (PDF p. 51). Definitions 1–4 in §2 specify source flattening and inclusion-maximal consistency in each priority prefix. Definition 11 in §5 takes every source's all/some-but-not-all/none individually consistent formulas. Strong stability means that every maxcon induces the same ranking. The three classes are source classes and the formula comparisons are strict inclusion at the first difference. The construction uses pairwise disjoint sources of at most three individually satisfiable propositional formulas. Simultaneous consistency of a source is outside this restriction and is not an assumption of the target model. Empty priority classes occur in the source's examples; the hard instances produce the candidate rankings with all three classes nonempty. [JAIR article](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/view/11238) · [Published PDF, §8.6](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/11238/26438#page=51) · [Pinned Q621](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/blob/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe/problems/computer_science_ai/theory-of-computation-algorithms-part-1.md#q621)
- Liberatore's Theorem 8 gives existence for two classes, and Theorem 17 gives explicit instances with no strongly stable tripartition. His later Lemma 11 realizes certain explicit transition graphs as source/maxcon structures, an antecedent to reasoning from designed maxcon outcomes. The reduction here produces a succinct propositional representation from a quantified formula. The bad modes remain maximal precisely when the chosen universal assignment has no repair. The source's related question on weakening the assumptions in Lemma 11 is outside the claimed result. [Published PDF, §5](https://www.jair.org/index.php/jair/article/download/11238/26438#page=23)

## Closest prior classifications

- Brewka, *Preferred Subtheories* (IJCAI 1989), §4, supplies the prior prefix-maximal consistency semantics for already stratified formulas. Coste-Marquis and Marquis, *On Stratified Belief Base Compilation* (AMAI 2004), §3.1, Proposition 6 and Table 1, summarize Π₂ᴾ-completeness for skeptical inference with the inclusion-preference policy on supplied strata. In those results the ranking is part of the input. Q621 first seeks a ranking that every preferred outcome induces again. [Brewka proceedings](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1989/167) · [Coste-Marquis and Marquis](https://www.cril.univ-artois.fr/~marquis/coste-marquis-amai04.pdf)
- Liberatore, *Belief merging in absence of reliability information* (Synthese 2022), studies all possible weights on sources. For binary-valued distance, its Theorem 1 uses Pareto dominance and obtains maxcon-based merging without a reliability ranking that must be induced again. This later work has a different selection rule. [Synthese paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11229-022-03750-7)

The adjacent Q622 skeptical-consequence classification uses the same base reduction and is a companion result, with additional reasoning for querying surviving maxcons. The construction is shared across the two records. [Q622 companion](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24572374)
