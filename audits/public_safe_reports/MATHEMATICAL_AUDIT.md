# Mathematical audit

Maximality in the final formula prefix implies that a maxcon contains every input formula consistent with it. Consequently every valuation realizing the maxcon has the same input truth set. Earlier prefixes distinguish possible dominating truth sets. If a formula occurs in multiple sources, comparing it in its earliest formula block gives the same prefixes and leaves the source-membership induction intact.

Padding the quantified matrix preserves the quantified truth value and gives at least one satisfying assignment to the matrix itself. The resulting sources are pairwise disjoint after syntactic tagging and every input formula is individually satisfiable. Every nonempty truth set has exactly the labels prescribed by its mode and selected variables.

For either candidate ranking, a matching outer assignment dominates all others in the first priority block. Different universal assignments are incomparable in the middle block. At the target ranking the two Bad modes tie on the middle block and are incomparable in the last block. Repair strictly improves the middle block, while Good is incomparable. At the rival ranking a matching Good remains undominated and induces the target ranking. These cases give both directions of the reduction.
