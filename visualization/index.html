How the cube compares modes

A vertex records which of the arm formulas A, A′ and C is present in the middle priority block. The Bad modes differ only in the last block, so they share a point. When Repair exists, it adds C to the middle block and dominates both. Good contains A′ and stays incomparable. Good and Repair induce the same tripartition.
